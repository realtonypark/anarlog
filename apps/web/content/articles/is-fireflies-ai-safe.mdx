"Fred from Fireflies" can join your call, record the conversation, and generate a recap. Before recording a sensitive meeting, check where that content goes and who can open it.

Fireflies documents safeguards against AI training and vendor retention, but those promises don't mean your meeting disappears after processing. Sharing permissions, storage, connected AI tools, and your organization's requirements still matter. I checked its current policies and help guides on **October 25, 2026**; here's how those boundaries work.

## What Is Fireflies AI Today?

![Fireflies' security page displaying SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and Zero Data Retention claims](anarlog/blog/articles/is-fireflies-ai-safe/fireflies-security-compliance.png)
*Fireflies' security and compliance claims. Source: [Fireflies security page](https://fireflies.ai/security).*


Fireflies is a cloud meeting assistant. Its "Fireflies.ai Notetaker" bot can join Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams calls to record, transcribe, and summarize them.

It also offers a **Google Meet SDK integration** and a **desktop "Take Notes" mode** for capture without a visible bot. These are different capture methods with different limits. In particular, [Live Assist works with both desktop bot capture and Take Notes](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7066927951-bot-vs-no-bot-which-capture-am-i-using); it isn't exclusive to the bot.

Removing the bot doesn't make Fireflies an on-device transcription service. Meeting content still goes through its cloud processing.

## What Fireflies Actually Collects

Fireflies' [privacy policy](https://fireflies.ai/privacy-policy) is dated **September 28, 2026**. It covers account and service-related personal information; business customers' meeting content is governed separately by their [terms of service](https://fireflies.ai/terms-of-service), [Data Processing Addendum](https://fireflies.ai/data-processing-agreement), and other agreements.

**From meetings:** audio and visual recordings, transcripts, summaries, participant names and emails, meeting titles and URLs.

**From your account:** name, company, email, phone number, physical address, and payment details.

**Automatically:** IP address, browser and device data, usage logs, and approximate location inferred from your IP.

**From integrations:** if you connect Google Calendar, Slack, or similar tools, Fireflies can access "any content that you have provided to and stored in your Third-Party Account", including contacts and calendar data, depending on the integration you enable.

Fireflies also discloses personal information to categories of third parties, per its state-privacy-law disclosures: vendors and service providers, integration partners, corporate affiliates, law enforcement when legally required, professional advisors, and parties involved in a corporate transaction such as an acquisition.

Check the permissions for the integrations you actually enable rather than assuming every connection has the same access.

## What Fireflies Has Fixed

Fireflies states that it **does not train AI models on personal information** and prohibits its vendors from doing so. Its terms also exclude using customer content to train or improve generative AI models.

For core meeting features, its [Zero Data Retention policy](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2154538358-policy-on-keeping-information-safe) means processing vendors cannot retain meeting audio, video, transcripts, or summaries after processing or use them for training. **This is a vendor-processing promise, not a promise that Fireflies deletes your meeting history immediately.** Its terms describe deletion after you remove content, with exceptions for copies in other users' accounts, backups, and legal obligations.

Account personal information follows a separate rule: the privacy policy says it is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of closure. Other personal information can be retained for the purposes described in the policy; don't apply the account timeline to every meeting copy or connected service.

**MCP has a separate boundary.** Fireflies' [MCP guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3039542843-learn-about-fireflies-mcp-server-connect-your-ai-tool?lang=en) says its ZDR promise does not cover data shared with external AI tools through MCP. The destination's retention and training terms apply. Review that tool before making meeting transcripts available to it.

Fireflies' [security page](https://fireflies.ai/security) lists SOC 2 Type II and describes GDPR and HIPAA controls. A vendor's compliance badges don't establish that every account configuration is suitable for your meeting. The Enterprise healthcare setup has additional requirements, covered below.

### Where Are Fireflies' Servers and Meeting Records?

For its Enterprise storage options, Fireflies' [Private Storage guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3687416644-learn-about-private-storage) says processing happens on U.S. servers even when data is stored in a designated bucket. It describes managed Private Storage on Google Cloud in US West Los Angeles and a separate bring-your-own-storage option with more customization. Storage location and processing location are different questions. Confirm both for your account and contract if data residency matters; choosing a storage bucket doesn't turn transcription into local processing.

## Where the Real Friction Still Is

![Fireflies' meeting recap sharing settings, with 'Teammates & anyone with the link' set as the default access level](anarlog/blog/library/products/fireflies/privacy/2026-09-22-recap-audience.png)
*Fireflies' meeting recap sharing settings. Source: [Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/).*


**Check who can open a recap, separately from who receives its email.** Fireflies' [sharing guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/5319481450-how-to-configure-privacy-settings-for-your-meeting-recaps) documents "Teammates & anyone with the link" as the default: workspace teammates can find the meeting in #AllMeetings, and a forwarded link can grant access. Available content for unauthenticated visitors depends on the plan and Public Meeting Access setting. Review your actual workspace default and each sensitive meeting's Share settings. [Recap email recipients and viewing permissions are separate controls](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/7339665361-how-to-configure-meeting-recap-emails-and-privacy-settings); sending fewer emails doesn't by itself restrict link access.

**The content license is broad but purpose-limited.** The terms describe a perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable license scoped to providing the service to you and authorized users. That license does not override the separate no-AI-training commitment.

**The standard terms limit liability, with exceptions.** Section 18 generally caps liability at the greater of $100 or the preceding six months' fees. Exceptions include gross negligence, fraud, intentional misconduct, and liabilities that applicable law doesn't allow the parties to exclude. Review your signed agreement rather than assuming the cap settles every possible claim.

**Recording consent remains your responsibility.** The terms require participant consent. A bot, recording banner, or enabled calendar integration doesn't replace meeting your organization's recording rules and applicable consent requirements.

**Enterprise alone isn't the full HIPAA setup.** Fireflies' [HIPAA instructions](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3704059205-set-up-hipaa-compliance-for-your-workspace?lang=en) require the Enterprise plan, enabled Private Storage, and a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA). A badge on the security page doesn't establish that those conditions are enabled for your workspace.

## The Bot-Free Option, and Its Limits

![The consent banner Fireflies shows when recording through its Google Meet SDK integration instead of sending a visible bot](anarlog/blog/articles/is-fireflies-ai-safe/fireflies-botfree-consent-banner.png)
*Fireflies' bot-free consent banner. Source: [Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/).*


Fireflies' [Google Meet SDK guide](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/3309351579-integrate-google-meet-sdk-with-fireflies-for-bot-free-meeting-recording) says capture starts after approval by a participant from the host's domain; other attendees can stop sharing. It captures up to three active participants' audio/video streams at a time and doesn't capture meeting chat or captions.

Desktop Take Notes has different limits: it creates a transcript and summary without saving a separate audio/video recording, and lacks named speaker labels and Sales Assist. [Live Assist is available in Take Notes](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1208704416-getting-started-with-the-fireflies-desktop-app?lang=en). Choose the capture method based on the record you need, then review where that record is processed and shared.

## Should You Trust Fireflies With Sensitive Information?

Use these checks before your first sensitive meeting:

1. **Approve the data path.** Confirm that your organization permits Fireflies, its processing locations, and any connected AI tools for this information. Legal and financial confidentiality requirements need their own review; a healthcare BAA doesn't answer them.
2. **Restrict the audience.** Consider "Only Owner" with explicit invitations for confidential meetings. Check workspace defaults, the meeting's Share settings, and recap email recipients separately.
3. **Set the retention expectations.** Confirm how long meeting records stay in your workspace, what deletion covers, and whether copies exist in other accounts or integrations.
4. **For healthcare workflows involving PHI, verify all three vendor prerequisites:** Enterprise, Private Storage, and an executed BAA. Have the responsible team approve the workflow before recording.
5. **Arrange participant consent.** Tell people what will be captured and shared before starting. Recheck automatic capture settings when the meeting audience or purpose changes.

## A Private Alternative: Anarlog

If you want an on-device option, our [local AI meeting notes guide](/blog/local-ai-meeting-notes/) explains how local speech and language models change the data path. That depends on configuration, not just a "local-first" label.

![Anarlog's meeting summary view](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg)

Anarlog is an open-source AI notepad for meetings that stores your canonical meeting record locally in SQLite instead of a vendor-hosted workspace. Markdown export is available when you want a portable copy. See our [Fireflies AI Alternatives](/blog/fireflies-ai-alternatives) article for a full Anarlog-vs-Fireflies comparison.

**Your choice of AI stack:** use hosted AI, bring your own API keys, or run local models via Ollama. Hosted or bring-your-own-key processing sends content to the provider you select; choose local models for on-device processing.

**Bot-free capture:** Anarlog captures system audio and microphone for Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, phone calls, or in-person conversations, without a bot joining and without needing a separate SDK integration per meeting platform.

**You control distribution:** export Markdown when you want a portable copy. Optional CloudSync and sharing have separate data paths; review those settings as well as your AI provider.

**What Anarlog itself collects, for the same transparency this article asks of Fireflies:** pseudonymous usage and diagnostic data, device and log data, whatever content you deliberately send to optional cloud features (hosted transcription, AI, sharing, or connectors), website analytics, and payment data processed by Stripe. Meeting audio, transcripts, notes, and summaries aren't part of Anarlog's own analytics or telemetry. [Read Anarlog's privacy policy](/privacy) for the full breakdown.

A local-only configuration reduces how much of your meeting content leaves your device, but it doesn't replace getting consent, securing your own device, or reviewing every cloud feature you turn on.

## Is Fireflies AI Safe?

Fireflies documents useful safeguards: no AI training on customer content, ZDR for its core processing vendors, and controls over meeting access. Whether it fits your meeting depends on your sharing configuration, storage and processing requirements, connected AI tools, and contractual obligations. Check those together before treating a bot-free mode or compliance badge as sufficient approval.
